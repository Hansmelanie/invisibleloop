Ask us directly →
How does a cheating spouse investigation work?

We start by listening to your concerns and learning what you need to know. If the request is appropriate and lawful, we agree on a clear scope, plan any observation, and provide a factual report of documented findings.

Can you access my partner’s WhatsApp or Telegram?

No. We don’t break into accounts, intercept messages, or bypass security. We can review records you are authorized to share and conduct other lawful investigative work.

What does social media OSINT include?

Open-source intelligence means structured research using publicly accessible content and records. It does not include accessing private accounts, evading access controls, or obtaining someone’s login credentials.

Will you tell me what you find?

Yes. If an investigation proceeds, the agreed scope includes factual reporting on the work completed. We don’t guarantee a particular outcome or make conclusions beyond the evidence.

Is the first conversation confidential?

We handle initial inquiries discreetly. Before sharing sensitive details, we’ll explain how your information is handled and any limits that apply under local law.

Where do you operate?

Coverage and licensing requirements vary by location. Tell us where the matter is based and we’ll confirm whether we can assist before any work begins.
